about

Hi, we're Jehna and Danielle.

Table Notes is our small collection of recipes, drinks, and places we want to try, revisit, or remember.

We created the site for our web design class, but we wanted it to feel personal to us rather than like a template. We kept the layout simple, with good photos, readable text, and pages that are easy to browse.

Most of what you will find here comes from things we genuinely like or are interested in, from comfort food and everyday drinks to places we would love to visit someday.

We are still learning as we build, so Table Notes is something we can continue improving and adding to over time.
